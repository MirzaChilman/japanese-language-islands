# Basketball

**Prompt:** バスケ好き？  
*Basuke suki?* — Do you like basketball?

**Japanese**  
うん、バスケ大好き！今度、試合を観に行くんだ。会場で観ると盛り上がるし、今から楽しみだよ。

**Romaji**  
Un, basuke daisuki! Kondo, shiai o mi ni ikun da. Kaijō de miru to moriagaru shi, ima kara tanoshimi da yo.

**English**  
Yeah, I love basketball! I’m going to watch a game soon. It’s exciting watching live, and I’m already looking forward to it.

**Follow-up:** どこのチームの試合を観に行くの？  
*Doko no chīmu no shiai o mi ni iku no?* — Which team are you going to watch?

**Reply pattern:** 〇〇の試合を観に行くよ。  
*___ no shiai o mi ni iku yo.* — I’m going to watch ___ play.
