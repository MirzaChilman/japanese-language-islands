# Snowboarding

**Prompt:** 冬は何してるの？  
*Fuyu wa nani shiteru no?* — What do you do in winter?

**Japanese**  
冬はよくスノボに行くよ。去年の冬に始めたばかりだけど、すっかりハマっちゃって。今シーズンは神立によく行く予定で、カービングをもっと練習したいんだ。

**Romaji**  
Fuyu wa yoku sunobo ni iku yo. Kyonen no fuyu ni hajimeta bakari da kedo, sukkari hamacchatte. Ima shīzun wa Kandatsu ni yoku iku yotei de, kābingu o motto renshū shitain da.

**English**  
I go snowboarding a lot in winter. I only started last winter, but I got hooked. This season I’m planning to go to Kandatsu often and work more on carving.

**Follow-up:** 神立のどこが好き？  
*Kandatsu no doko ga suki?* — What do you like about Kandatsu?
